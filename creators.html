What the book is about

Most challenges pair a thinking task with movement. One minute a child is spotting what changed in a picture or finishing a pattern; the next, they are crossing hands to opposite knees or balancing on one foot. No screens — mostly a pencil and a little floor space; a few pages use small things like beads or building blocks.

  • 60 brain + body challenges, about 10 minutes each
  • Two levels in every activity: Ages 6–7 Just Play and Ages 8–10 Level Up! — siblings can share one book
  • Spot the difference, patterns, shape grids, mini-sudoku, mazes and map planning, memory games, number tracks
  • Cross-body moves, left- and right-hand tasks, balance challenges
  • Answer keys at the back for every puzzle with a right answer
  • A certificate of completion at the end

About the author

Natalia Milos is the director of Coddy, a coding school for kids in New York, and has worked with children for more than 10 years. Her background spans education, youth fitness and brain fitness; she holds the Youth Exercise Specialist (YES) certificate from NASM and the Brain Fitness Coach certification from ISSA.
